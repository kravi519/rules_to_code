Change-Approval Evidence Pack — INTCALC modernisation

1. Change Summary

This pack covers a proposed reimplementation of INTCALC against 12 SME-validated business rules, tested against 23 vectors (20 of 23 passing).

2. Test Evidence

20 of 23 vectors matched the oracle exactly; 3 field-level divergence(s) were found and are itemised in the Known Divergences section below, over this stated, finite vector set.

3. Impact Assessment

Traceable rule coverage is 100.0%; every validated rule is cited by at least one test.

4. Known Divergences

Vector R-004-v1 (rule R-004, field 'interest'): expected 1500.00, got 2750.0. Vector R-006-v1 (rule R-006, field 'interest'): expected 10.13, got 10.12. Vector R-008-v2 (rule R-008, field 'balance_magnitude'): expected -4567, got 4567.

5. Rollback Plan

Untraceable behaviour was found on field 'capped_interest' (example vector R-003-v2): capped_interest differs from interest for this vector, but no validated rule's `fields` list documents capped_interest. This is code behaviour no rule explains.
